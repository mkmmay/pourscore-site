Free guide

How to pour a heart

A free guide to your first latte art heart, in three moves.

  1. 01Lay the baseFill the cup to about 45% first.
  2. 02Drop in close, speed upTilt the cup, spout almost touching, and drop in about a centimetre in from the back. Stay in that one spot and pour faster as it fills.
  3. 03Lift and pull throughSlow down just before it is full, lift high and draw one fast line through the middle.

In Pour Score the path is drawn across your cup and timed for you.
