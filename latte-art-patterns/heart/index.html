How to pour a latte art heart

A monk's head with a tail pulled through it. One drop in, flow rate climbing, then up and through.

In Pour Score the heart sits in the Home Pourer tier.

Steps

  1. Lay the base. Swirl the crema away first, tilt the cup a little and start low in the middle. Rise 2 to 3 cm and move gently side to side until the cup is about 45% full. Stay off the edges, or foam rides up the wall and shows white.
  2. Drop in close, speed up. Tilt the cup, spout almost touching, and drop in about a centimetre in from the back. Stay in that one spot and pour faster as it fills.
  3. Lift and pull through. Slow down just before it is full, lift high and draw one fast line through the middle.

If it goes wrong

Fat line through the middle means you stayed too close for the pull-through. Go back up high and draw faster, speed makes the line thin and straight.
