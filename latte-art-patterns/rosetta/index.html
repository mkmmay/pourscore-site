How to pour a latte art rosetta

The heart plus a wiggle. Small, slow, even rocking while the milk does the spreading for you.

In Pour Score the rosetta sits in the Confident tier.

Steps

  1. Lay the base. Swirl the crema away first, tilt the cup a little and start low in the middle. Rise 2 to 3 cm and move gently side to side until the cup is about 60% full. Stay off the edges, or foam rides up the wall and shows white.
  2. Drop in the middle, then wiggle. Stay high over the base, then come down close in the middle of the cup and start rocking straight away. Keep the jug in the middle while the leaves push away from you, then zigzag back to the near rim.
  3. Up and through. At the near rim, hold a beat for the small heart, then lift high and draw one line back through every leaf.

If it goes wrong

Leaves thin and cramped? Your pour slowed down while you concentrated on rocking, that is the fight in this pattern. Fat stem? You stayed close for the pull-through instead of lifting high.
