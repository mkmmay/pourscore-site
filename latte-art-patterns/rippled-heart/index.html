How to pour a latte art rippled heart

One heart, layered from the inside. Drop in, keep the spout on one spot and rock gently so the milk ripples out in rings, fill the centre, then pull through.

In Pour Score the rippled heart sits in the Confident tier.

Steps

  1. Lay the base. Swirl the crema away first, tilt the cup a little and start low in the middle. Rise 2 to 3 cm and move gently side to side until the cup is about 50% full. Stay off the edges, or foam rides up the wall and shows white.
  2. Drop in, then ripple. Drop in close, just past the centre. Once the white shows, rock the jug gently side to side, keeping the spout close and almost still.
  3. Up and through. When the cup is almost level, lift a little and draw one line through the heart.

If it goes wrong

Rings smeared into one blob? The jug is travelling. Keep the spout on one spot and let only the milk move. Lopsided rings? Rock the same small amount each way.
