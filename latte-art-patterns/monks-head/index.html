How to pour a latte art monk's head

A heart without the tail. One drop, no wiggle, the shape every other pattern is built on.

In Pour Score the monk's head sits in the Beginner tier.

Steps

  1. Lay the base. Swirl the crema away first, tilt the cup a little and start low in the middle. Rise 2 to 3 cm and move gently side to side until the cup is about 30% full. Stay off the edges, or foam rides up the wall and shows white.
  2. One drop, speed up. Drop in near the back of the tilted cup and pour faster and faster.

If it goes wrong

Shape stuck in the middle of the cup means you poured too gently. Speed up as it fills and let the milk push itself out to the wall.
