1. Who we are

Pour Score is a latte art coaching application operated by Marlon Kazim May, trading as Pour Score ("we", "us", "our"), a sole trader. We are based in the United Kingdom.

We are the data controller for personal data collected through the Pour Score app. This means we decide how and why your data is processed.

For questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at legal@pourscoreapp.com. We aim to respond within 5 working days.

2. What data we collect and why

We collect only the data we need to operate the app and provide you with coaching and community features. Below is every category of data we collect, why we collect it, and the legal basis under UK GDPR.

2a. Account data

What: Email address, display name, username (handle), profile photograph (if you add one).

Why: To create and manage your account, authenticate you, and display your profile to other users.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract — you need an account to use Pour Score.

2b. Equipment profile data

What: Coffee machine brand and model, grinder brand and model, milk jug brand, model and volume, cup brand, model and volume, preferred milk type.

Why: To personalise AI coaching feedback and pattern guidance to your specific setup.

Legal basis: Legitimate interests — personalised coaching is the core purpose of the app and users reasonably expect this personalisation.

2c. Pour photographs

What: Photographs of your latte art that you capture or upload within the app.

Why: To provide AI-powered scoring and coaching feedback. Photographs you choose to post are also displayed to other users on the community feed.

Legal basis: Consent — you actively initiate each AI analysis and each community post. You can withdraw consent by not posting and by deleting existing posts.

Important: Pour photographs submitted for AI analysis are transmitted to OpenAI (see Section 4). Photographs you post publicly are visible to other Pour Score users. Photographs you do not post remain private.

2d. Pour log and progress data

What: Your pour history, AI scores across dimensions (symmetry, texture, contrast, definition), comfort scores (your private 1–5 self-rating), AI coaching notes, tier level and accuracy, streak count and personal best.

Why: To track your progress over time and deliver personalised coaching insights.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract — progress tracking is a core feature of the app.

Privacy note: AI dimension scores, comfort scores, coaching notes, and your last pour date are private to you. Only your tier, streak, and pour count are visible to other users.

2e. Community and social data

What: Posts you publish (photographs, captions), comments you write, likes you give, ratings you submit, clubs you join or create, users you follow.

Why: To operate the community feed and social features of the app.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract — community features are part of the service you signed up for.

2f. Device and technical data

What: Device orientation sensor data (tilt angle, used for the guided pour feature only), device type and operating system version, app version, push notification token.

Why: The tilt sensor data powers the real-time cup angle guide during a guided pour session. It is processed locally on your device and is not stored or transmitted. Device and OS data is used for technical support and to ensure compatibility. Push notification tokens are used to send you coaching results and streak reminders.

Legal basis: Legitimate interests for technical data. Consent for push notifications — you can withdraw this at any time in your device settings.

2g. Authentication data

What: If you sign in with Apple, Google, or email and password, we receive the information provided by that method. For Apple Sign In and Google Sign In, this is typically a unique identifier and, optionally, your email address. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

Why: To authenticate you securely without storing passwords ourselves where third-party sign-in is used.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract.

2h. The scoring library

What: Unless you turn it off, copies of the pour photographs you submit for AI scoring, together with the pattern you chose, your comfort score, the AI scores, and any rating given by Pour Score or a Pour Score coach. We store these copies without your name, username or email address.

Why: To build a library of real pours that helps AI scoring become more accurate and fairer over time. We use it to show the AI scoring system real examples of each pattern to compare new pours against, to set the score each level asks for, and to check the accuracy of our scoring.

Legal basis: Legitimate interests. Accurate, fair scoring is the core of the service you signed up for, and a library of real, anonymised pours is how we improve it. We have assessed that this does not unduly affect your privacy, because the copies carry no name or account details, are never shown to anyone, and are never used for anything other than improving scoring. The library is on by default, and we tell you about it when you sign up. You can turn it off at any time in your profile settings (your right to object, Section 6), and we remove your photographs from the library within 30 days. Turning it off does not affect your own scores, your pour log or your account. It also does not undo accuracy checks or level scores we have already worked out from the library as a whole, because those contain no photographs or personal data.

Important: Library photographs are never shown to other users, never sold, and never used to train OpenAI's or anyone else's AI models. When AI scoring compares a pour against library examples, those example photographs are sent to OpenAI in the same way as your own photographs (see Section 4). We do not add photographs that show a person's face to the library.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect or process:

  • Your precise GPS location
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Payment card details (handled entirely by Apple's payment system via RevenueCat — we never see your card number)
  • Any biometric data
  • Any data about your device's other apps or browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers (we do not run ads in V1 of the app)

4. Who we share your data with

We share data only where necessary to operate the app. We do not sell your data to anyone.

OpenAI (AI analysis)

When you request AI scoring of a pour photograph, that photograph is transmitted securely to OpenAI, L.L.C. via their API. OpenAI processes the image to generate your coaching scores and feedback, then returns the result to us. Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, images submitted via the API are not used to train OpenAI's models. OpenAI retains API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for abuse-monitoring purposes before deletion, unless a longer period is required by law.

Where the scoring library is in use (Section 2h), a small number of library photographs may be sent alongside a pour being scored, as examples for comparison. They are sent without names or any other account details, and they are handled under the same OpenAI terms.

OpenAI is our data processor for this purpose, acting under our instructions. For more information on OpenAI's data practices, see openai.com/enterprise-privacy.

Supabase (database and file storage)

Your account data, pour log, progress data, and community content are stored on servers operated by Supabase, Inc. Our Supabase project is hosted in the UK (London region). Supabase acts as our data processor and processes data only on our instructions. For more information, see supabase.com/privacy.

Expo (push notifications)

Push notifications are delivered via Expo's notification infrastructure. To send you a notification, your device push token is transmitted to Expo's servers. Expo acts as our data processor for this purpose. For more information, see expo.dev/privacy.

RevenueCat (subscription management)

When you start a trial or subscription, your subscription status and purchase history are managed by RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat does not receive your payment card details — those are handled directly by Apple. RevenueCat acts as our data processor. For more information, see revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple (Sign In with Apple, payments, App Store)

If you use Sign In with Apple, Apple processes your authentication. If you make an in-app purchase, Apple processes your payment. Apple acts as an independent data controller for these purposes under its own privacy policy. For more information, see apple.com/privacy.

Legal and safety disclosures

We may disclose your data if required to do so by law, court order, or regulatory authority, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Pour Score, our users, or the public.

6. Your rights under UK GDPR

As a UK resident, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right to access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to rectification: You can correct inaccurate data through your profile settings, or contact us to correct data you cannot edit yourself.

Right to erasure: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time using the Delete Account button in your profile settings. We will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Right to restrict processing: You can ask us to pause processing your data in certain circumstances.

Right to data portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used format.

Right to object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests. For the scoring library (Section 2h), you do not need to contact us: turn it off in your profile settings and we always stop.

Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on your consent (AI analysis, push notifications), you can withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@pourscoreapp.com. We will respond within one month. We will not charge you for requests unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data:

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption (TLS)
  • Data stored in Supabase is encrypted at rest by default
  • Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain), not in plain text
  • Access to our database is controlled by Row Level Security policies — each user can only access their own private data
  • API keys and server credentials are stored as server-side secrets and never included in the app's code

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at legal@pourscoreapp.com.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and notify affected users without undue delay, as required by UK GDPR.

8. Children

Pour Score is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at legal@pourscoreapp.com and we will delete it promptly.

If you are between 13 and 17, please ensure a parent or guardian has reviewed this policy.

Where data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the UK ICO (the International Data Transfer Agreement, IDTA) or adequacy decisions where applicable.
