How it works

From first heart to confident pour.

01

Pick a pattern, read the plan

Every pattern is broken into chapters: what to do with your height, your flow and your motion, in the order you do it. Start with Milk First, then your first heart.

  • Dry rehearsal to practise the motion with no milk
