7. AI scoring

Pour Score uses AI technology to analyse pour photographs and provide coaching feedback. You should understand the following about how AI scoring works:

Scores are coaching feedback, not professional assessment. AI scores reflect the system's analysis of visual characteristics in your photograph. They are intended to guide your practice, not to certify or formally assess your barista skills.

Scores may vary. Image quality, lighting, angle, and other factors may affect scores between pours. Consistency in how you photograph your pours will produce more consistent results.

Community ratings are separate. Community ratings reflect the views of other Pour Score users, not Pour Score's assessment of your work. We do not endorse or verify community ratings.

No appeal process for individual scores. You may request AI re-analysis of a pour or supplement your score with a community rating, but we do not manually review or adjust individual AI scores.
